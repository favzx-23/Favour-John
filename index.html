About me

I design visuals people stop and look at.

I'm Favour John, a graphic designer and the founder of Favz Graphix, my design studio. I work with startups, tech companies and small businesses on logos, brand identities, flyers, menus and social media graphics.

I've designed social media campaigns for Futurelabs, a full brand system for my own studio, and print and promo work for clients in food, grooming and events. I care about clear layouts, strong type and designs that are ready to print or post on time.
