About me

I design visuals people stop and look at.

I'm Favour John, a graphic designer and the founder of Favz Graphix, my design studio. I work with startups, tech companies and small businesses on logos, brand identities, flyers, menus and social media graphics.

I design the social media for Futurelabs, a tech hub in Akwa Ibom: monthly campaigns, event posters and programme launches. I've also built identities for hotels, catering and service companies. I care about clear layouts, strong type and designs that are ready to print or post on time.
